Sweet Basil

Ocimum basilicum • Annual

About Basil

Basil is an aromatic culinary herb native to tropical regions from Central Africa to Southeast Asia. It is widely used in Italian and Mediterranean cuisine.

Kitchen Uses

Best known as the primary ingredient in Genovese pesto. Fresh basil pairs naturally with ripe tomatoes, olive oil, garlic, and fresh pasta dishes.

Growing Tips

Basil loves warm temperatures, direct sunlight (6 to 8 hours daily), and well-drained soil. Water at the base of the plant to keep the leaves dry.
